# Istante

> Istante is an Italian technology company (VAT IT03635230547, Perugia area) that develops and operates Athena, a business-process-automation platform built around a team of specialized AI assistants. Athena spans assistants for communication routing and email classification, credit management and collections, a 24/7 voice/phone assistant, quote and order processing, automated tender monitoring and scoring, outbound lead engagement, contract and legal analysis, and HR and onboarding. It is offered on a fixed-cost "Digital Working Units" (DWU) pricing model, integrates across email, Microsoft Teams, WhatsApp, SMS, and phone, keeps critical decisions under human oversight, and is presented as GDPR and EU AI Act compliant.
